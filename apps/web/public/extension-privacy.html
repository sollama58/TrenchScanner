Trench Overlay privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026

Trench Overlay is TrenchScanner's Chrome extension. It shows your own TrenchScanner alerts in a side panel. This page explains what it handles and why.

What it can't see

The extension never runs on any website. It has no access to the pages you visit, including Axiom, Terminal (Padre) and GMGN. It can't read their content, cookies, logins or wallets, and it doesn't record your browsing history. Its only Chrome permissions are storage and the side panel. It has no access to any website, and the only server it talks to is api.trenchscanner.app.

What it stores, on your computer only

  • Your extension key, which TrenchScanner Settings creates for this browser. It is used as an authentication token.
  • A shortened form of your wallet address, shown in the panel's header.
  • Your overlay settings: which alerts and flags to show, and which sites to link to.

These stay in Chrome's extension storage on your device. Disconnecting in the panel or uninstalling the extension removes them.

What it sends, and to whom

  • To TrenchScanner (api.trenchscanner.app): your key, to read your recent alerts, token stats and overlay settings, and to save those settings when you change them. TrenchScanner records when each key was last used, so you can see it in Settings.
  • Token images load from where the token's launcher or DexScreener hosts them, for example cdn.dexscreener.com or images.pump.fun. Those hosts see your IP address, as they would if you opened the image on TrenchScanner's site.

The extension sends nothing else to anyone. It has no analytics, no tracking and no ads. Your data is not sold or shared, and it is not used for anything except showing you your own alerts.

Your key

A key can read only your alerts, token stats and overlay settings, and save the overlay settings. It can't trade, move funds, see wallets or change your account. You can revoke any key at any time in TrenchScanner Settings, under Browser extension. Signing out everywhere revokes them all.

Changes

If what the extension handles changes, this page is updated first and the date above changes with it.
